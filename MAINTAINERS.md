# Maintainers

The people who have access to the sensitive resources of the project (the GitHub repository and
organization, and, from the first release, the PyPI project `sankey-studio`).

| Role | Who | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Owner and lead maintainer | Giovanni Manzoni ([@giovannimanzoni](https://github.com/giovannimanzoni)) | Owner of the `Axiumine` organization, admin of the repository, owner of the PyPI project |
| Co-owner and successor | [@vivi7](https://github.com/vivi7) | Owner of the `Axiumine` organization, admin of the repository |

Both accounts use two-factor authentication, which the organization requires. Access to the PyPI project and
to the approval of a release is held by the lead maintainer; adding the co-owner is described in
[docs/release.md](docs/release.md). The roles and how
decisions are made are in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
